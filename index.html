Customer Sales Analytics Dashboard ↗
The Problem
A company had 5,000+ sales records spread across multiple sheets with no visibility into which products drove profit, which customer segments were underperforming, or where revenue was leaking. Decision-makers were flying blind.
5,000+ records processed
Pareto analysis applied
Customer segmentation built
Margin gaps identified
The Approach
Applied SQL joins and aggregations to clean and structure the raw transactional data. Performed customer segmentation to identify the top 20% of customers driving 80% of revenue. Used DAX in Power BI to calculate dynamic KPIs and built an executive dashboard with drill-down capability by region, product, and time.
Business Impact: Identified underperforming product segments representing ~23% of SKUs with negative margin contribution. Recommendations supported margin optimisation decisions for management.
